Project 01 / May 2026

19 m² Apartment Renovation

Project Brief

Transform a 19-square-metre apartment in central Stockholm into a functional, personal and visually cohesive home, while preserving the character of the original 18th-century architecture.

Location & Period

Central Stockholm / 2026

Spatial Attributes

Two large windows, 3.5-metre-high ceilings, original period character.

Budget & Scope

SEK 250,000 total renovation budget with a complete rework of a compact footprint.

Design Focus

Open-plan functionality, comfort, material cohesion and a sense of spaciousness.

Layout & Planning

Spatial Layout & Structural Changes

Wall Demolition & Floor Plan

To maximize the 19 m², I removed the wall leading to the former pantry and stripped away the old vinyl flooring. The original pantry was cramped, lacked an oven, and offered minimal counter space. Opening up the layout transformed the workflow, allowing natural light to flow freely and laying the foundation for an open-plan kitchen with the refined ambiance of a boutique hotel suite.

Function & Detail Planning

My planning focused on creating a clear, balanced layout that felt generous and highly functional. Every decision was made to improve flow, storage and comfort while keeping the room visually calm and uncluttered.

Kitchen Planning

Function & Detail Planning

Designed in IKEA Planner and refined in Canva, the layout optimizes every millimeter without feeling cluttered. I integrated full-sized functionality including a dishwasher, combi-oven, integrated storage, and strategically placed sockets to avoid visible cables. A custom window seat was crafted to maximize seating while framing the light from the windows.

Kitchen Details

Lighting & Details

Scandinavian Classics & Vintage

I installed two small green shoemaker-style lamps—a classic Scandinavian design element. To pair with the lighting, I sourced a second-hand 1950s-style mirror with decorative brass studs, crafted by a Swedish furniture manufacturer.

Integrated Shelving

A glass shelf was added in alignment with the cooker hood to integrate the wall visually. Choosing glass introduces a subtle modern element while allowing ambient light from the lamps to shine through seamlessly.

Living Area

Back to where it all began

Having established the kitchen, the vision for the main living area focused on restoring timeless period character. I selected dark herringbone parquet flooring, high period-style wall panelling, vintage electrical fittings, and ornate ceiling roses to create a refined, cohesive space.

Concept

Visualization & Inspiration

To visualize the living area, the spatial layout was modeled in IKEA’s planning tool, with custom wall sconces and ambient lighting layered in Canva. Reference images below highlight the intended architectural elements: high wall panelling, dark herringbone flooring, and intricate ceiling mouldings.

Project 02 / Fall 2024

Lounge Revitalisation

The Challenge

Located in a commercial property, this lounge was originally designed as a communal meeting point for building tenants. However, due to cold acoustics, stark lighting, and a lack of tactile warmth, the space remained underutilized.

Objective & Scope

I independently initiated, pitched, and presented a redesign proposal to the property manager. The goal was to transform the stark lounge into an inviting, harmonious hub through strategic material curation, acoustic management, and warm accent lighting.

Material Curation

Furniture & Material Curation

Metal Coffee Table

Selected to complement the metal elements in the existing seating area.

Walnut & Polished Coffee Table

A natural material that creates a calm feel and complements the room's lighting.

Textiles

Curated to create a calm, softer atmosphere—including two yellow cushions, one green cushion, one dark grey cushion, a light green rug, and a purple pouffe.

Yellow Table Lamp

Added to introduce an additional light source and complement the yellow cushions.

Outcome

The Result

Acoustic & Visual Transformation

By introducing a large textured rug and soft furnishings, the room's harsh acoustic bounce was mitigated. The cold white wall was repainted in a warmer tone to frame the seating area, creating a clear visual anchor.

Functional Success

The addition of warm wood textures, rich accent tones, and layered lighting successfully converted an unused transitional area into a welcoming, highly frequented tenant lounge.
